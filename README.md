Entry for the [make.fun bounty news-image-20261124](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124):
sum up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image.

The front page leads with the day's biggest story — COP31 ends with no
deal — and nine more stories from across the world: Ukraine's claim of
sinking a Russian warship off Libya, the factory fire in Indonesia, a
Russian cargo ship ablaze in the Gulf of Finland, strikes near Kyiv,
NATO's space defence pledge, Alcaraz's first ATP Finals title, the
World Chess Championship opening in Geneva, the UK hosting the 2035
Women's World Cup, and the UN court's genocide ruling on Myanmar.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
the output directory. Run it with:

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
